Parking Lot Guidelines & Safety Rules

Official parking regulations for all floor levels (Ground, B1, B2)
Mandatory Compliance
Rule 01 10 KM/H Speed Limit

Strict maximum speed limit of 10 km/h inside all ramps and drive lanes. Pedestrians always have right of way.

Rule 02 Park Strictly in Designated Bay

Park only within the white/yellow demarcated lines of your booked green slot. No blocking pathways or double parking.

Rule 03 EV Charging Bays Priority

Cyan slots are equipped with 50kW DC Superchargers. Non-EV vehicles parked in charging bays will be towed.

Rule 04 Digital Pass & QR Validation

Always keep your digital pass QR barcode ready for automatic boom barrier opening at entry and exit gates.

Rule 05 Track Time & Extend Before Expiry

Monitor your live parking timer on the Active Pass tab. Extend your reservation in advance to avoid penalty rates.

Rule 06 24/7 CCTV & Security Assistance

Premises are under continuous CCTV surveillance. For assistance, contact Floor Attendant or dial 1800-PARK-HELP.
